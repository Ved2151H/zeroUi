Data protection

What is recorded. When a device raises an emergency this system records the responsible user's device identifier, GPS fixes, DIGIPIN, device and sensor telemetry, a timestamped event trail, and audio captured by the device microphone. Nothing is recorded unless an emergency session is active; a cancelled countdown creates no record at all.

Who can see it. Access requires an authenticated authority session. Every incident, audio segment and realtime channel is scoped to a single emergency identifier, so an operator viewing one incident cannot retrieve another user's location or audio.

How long it is kept. Location, telemetry and audio are retained in the storage configured by the deploying authority (ZEROUI_DATA_DIR) until that authority deletes them. This console never deletes incident data; deletion is a deliberate administrative action.

Operator responsibility. Emergency audio and precise location are sensitive personal data. Use them only for the incident being responded to, and follow your organisation's handling policy.
